ResumeSkills is a curated collection of 20 AI agent skills designed to supercharge how Claude Code and other AI assistants handle resume writing, job searching, and career development. Skills are markdown files that you install once — they give your AI agent specialized knowledge, proven frameworks, and step-by-step workflows for every stage of a job search. Instead of asking your AI to "write a resume" and hoping for the best, ResumeSkills teaches it exactly how a professional career coach would approach ATS optimization, bullet writing, job matching, interview prep, and salary negotiation.

## What Are Skills?

Skills are structured markdown files that live in your AI agent's context. Each skill file contains:

- **Trigger conditions** — the keywords and task descriptions that tell the AI when to activate the skill
- **Core frameworks** — proven methodologies drawn from hiring best practices (e.g., the STAR method for interview answers, the XYZ formula for bullet points)
- **Step-by-step processes** — the exact sequence the AI follows to complete the task correctly
- **Output requirements** — formatting rules, length guidelines, and quality checklists
- **Before/after examples** — real transformations that calibrate the AI's output quality

When you describe a task — "optimize my resume for ATS" or "help me prep for a Google interview" — your AI assistant recognizes the relevant skill and immediately applies its specialized workflow. You get the output of an expert, not a generalist guess.

## The Problem ResumeSkills Solves

The modern job search is stacked against candidates. The numbers are sobering:

- **75% of resumes** are rejected by Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) before a human ever reads them — usually because of formatting issues or missing keywords
- **250 applications** flood the average job posting, making standing out a genuine challenge
- **Most resumes** rely on weak, duty-focused bullets with no metrics or measurable impact — exactly what hiring managers skip over
- **Job seekers** routinely apply to roles that are a poor fit, burning time and energy on applications that will never convert

The result is a job search that takes months longer than it should, offers that come in below market rate, and interview pipelines that never fill. The problem isn't that candidates lack qualifications — it's that their materials don't communicate those qualifications effectively.

## The Solution

ResumeSkills equips your AI assistant to close those gaps with precision. With the full skill set installed, you can:

- **Pass ATS filters** by optimizing resume formatting, keyword density, and section structure to match what parsing software expects
- **Stand out with achievement-focused bullets** that follow the XYZ formula — "accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z" — turning generic duties into compelling proof points
- **Apply strategically** by analyzing job descriptions for match scores, red flags, and skill gaps before you invest time tailoring an application
- **Get interviews faster** with tailored resumes, personalized cover letters, and thorough interview prep that turns callbacks into offers
